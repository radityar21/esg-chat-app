## SECTION TITLE (MANDATORY)
title: "3.3 Climate Scenario Analysis & Resilience"

## FRAMEWORKS ADDRESSED
- IFRS S2 Paragraph 22 — Climate resilience and scenario analysis
- ESRS E1 (SBM-3 / IRO-1) — Resilience of strategy to climate change; scenario analysis
- OJK PSPK 2 — Analisis skenario iklim (1,5°C, 2°C dan skenario yang lebih tinggi)

## DATA INPUT
Use ONLY the `scenario_analysis` object in DATA INPUT. It contains three scenarios (1.5°C, 2°C, 3°C+). For each
scenario the fields are:
  - scenario, carbon_price_usd_per_t
  - transition_delta_ecl_tn   (IDR trillion, additional expected credit loss from transition risk)
  - physical_loss_tn          (IDR trillion, loss on property collateral from physical risk)
  - green_opportunity_tn      (IDR trillion, incremental net interest income from green financing)
  - net_impact_tn             (IDR trillion)
  - downside_pct_of_loans     (percent of total loans)
  - by_sector[]               (sector, exposure_tn, ebitda_hit_pct, delta_ecl_tn)
Also available: `scenario_analysis.method`, `scenario_analysis.assumptions`, `scenario_analysis.horizon_years`.

## CONDITIONAL DISCLOSURE LOGIC
IF `scenario_analysis` is present:
  → Generate the FULL disclosure below.
IF it is missing or empty:
  → State that scenario analysis has not yet been performed, state the framework requirement (IFRS S2 ¶22,
    ESRS E1, PSPK 2: at least 1.5°C, 2°C and a higher-warming scenario), and give a roadmap. Do not invent numbers.

## REQUIRED ELEMENTS

### 1. Scope and approach (2 paragraphs)
- The three scenarios, their carbon-price pathways, the time horizon, and what the model covers (transition risk on
  the lending book, physical risk on property collateral, green-finance opportunity).
- State that the model is a screening model and quote `scenario_analysis.method`.

### 2. Results table (MANDATORY)
| Scenario | Carbon price (USD/tCO2e) | Transition ΔECL (IDR tn) | Physical loss (IDR tn) | Green opportunity (IDR tn) | Net impact (IDR tn) |
Use the values from DATA INPUT exactly as given. One row per scenario.

### 3. Sector concentration (1 paragraph + table)
- Name the three sectors with the largest `delta_ecl_tn` in the 1.5°C scenario, with their `delta_ecl_tn` values.

### 4. Resilience conclusion (1 paragraph, forward_looking)
- Qualitative conclusion about resilience of the strategy. Do NOT compare to risk appetite or claim adequacy unless a
  statement to that effect is in DATA INPUT. Use the qualifier "subject to market conditions".

### 5. Limitations and assumptions (methodology paragraph)
- List the assumptions from `scenario_analysis.assumptions`. Say that parameters are illustrative screening values
  and should be replaced by internal credit-risk models.
